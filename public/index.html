ProfileWho you'd be hiring

Payments are unforgiving. That's the part I like.

A card tap either clears or it doesn't. There's no half-working transaction, no graceful degradation when a terminal loses power mid-authorisation. Most of my career has been spent in that constraint: EMV flows, NFC readers, POS hardware and the fintech apps sitting on top of them.

That work taught me to build for the bad day rather than the demo. Offline queues, retry semantics, tight error states, releases that can be rolled back. I bring the same instinct to the newer things I'm shipping now, including AI features in mobile products and Go services behind them.

I'm most useful to a team that needs a senior mobile engineer who can talk to the payments people, the backend people and the people who actually use the app.
